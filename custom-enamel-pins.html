Hard Enamel Pins

Enamel is polished flush with the metal die, giving a smooth, glass-like surface. Best for premium merch, corporate gifts and collector pins where detail and durability matter.

Ideal for: brand merch, awards, high-end giveaways.

Soft Enamel Pins

Enamel sits below raised metal lines for a textured, tactile finish. The most popular and budget-friendly option, with vivid colours and fast production.

Ideal for: events, fundraisers, large runs.

Lapel Pins & Brooches

Elegant wearable accents with secure clutches — butterfly, rubber or deluxe magnetic. Perfect for uniforms, clubs, milestones and fashion.

Ideal for: uniforms, membership, gifts.

Custom Badges

Round, square or fully custom shapes in enamel or button style. Great for schools, promotions, awareness campaigns and staff identification.

Ideal for: events, schools, promo.
